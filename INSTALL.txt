SHOWCALL — INSTALL ON YOUR PHONE

Your phone can only install a web app from an https:// address, so this folder
first goes on a free host. Pick one:

OPTION A — Netlify Drop (easiest, about 2 minutes, from a computer)
 1. Unzip Showcall-install.zip.
 2. Go to app.netlify.com/drop and drag the "showcall-install" folder onto the page.
 3. It gives you an address like https://something.netlify.app — open it on your phone.

OPTION B — GitHub Pages (your danasmithog account)
 1. Create a repository, upload everything in this folder.
 2. Settings > Pages > Deploy from branch > main > Save.
 3. Open https://danasmithog.github.io/Showcall-/app.html on your phone.

THEN INSTALL
 Android (Chrome): open the address > menu (three dots) > "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
 iPhone (Safari): open the address > Share > "Add to Home Screen".

The Showcall icon appears on your home screen. It opens full screen, starts on the
app, and keeps working without signal after the first open. Long-press the icon
for shortcuts to the Owner portal and the website.

Note: demo data lives on each phone only. Real shared chat, logins, watch alerts
and integrations need the hosted backend and the Android/watch apps.
