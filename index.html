    Rules built in, not settings

    Some things should never be up to an app.

    Allergens stay word for word

    Paraphrases are rejected in code. There's no edit button on a dietary restriction.

    Codes in a private vault

    Door, parking and ice codes are encrypted, never exported and never shown on a watch.

    People decide

    No auto-sending, auto-accepting shifts or auto-buying. Every conflict waits for a person.

    No guessing

    If a time, code or name isn't in a source, Showcall says UNKNOWN.

    Location only on the clock

    Venue zones only, opt-in, pausable, wiped after the event. Never used to rate anyone.

    Every decision logged

    Flags, approvals, vault reveals and exports all land in the audit log.

    FAQ

    Questions event companies ask first

    Does it replace Nowsta or our scheduling tool?

    No. Your scheduling tool stays the system of record for rosters, shift offers, clock-in for pay and payroll. Showcall reads staffing in and runs the night itself. It never accepts, declines or offers a shift.

    Which watches work?

    Wear OS watches (Android) and watches running AsteroidOS (open-source Linux). Cues first arrive as phone notifications the watch shows; the native watch apps add the full-screen pause and one-tap acknowledge. Crew without a watch get the same cues on their phone.

    Do crew have to pay or buy anything?

    No. The crew app is free for everyone working a Showcall event. A watch is optional.

    What documents can it read?

    BEOs, pack lists and staffing sheets as PDFs today, starting with the formats we've proven on real events. Other formats are added as we onboard each company.

    Is my crew being tracked?

    Only while they're clocked in and only if they agree — and only which venue zone they're in, never GPS. They can pause it, it stops at clock-out, and it's wiped when the event closes.

    What if the venue has bad signal?

    The night's cue schedule is copied to phones and watches before doors so cues still fire offline. That offline mode is on the roadmap for early access.

    What's ready today and what's coming?

    Ready in early access: document intake, conflict flags, the event record, private vault, run of show, running-late changes, crew app, manager view and owner portal. Rolling out: native Wear OS and AsteroidOS apps, voice on the watch, live crew locator with venue zone tags, and integrations like Nowsta and accounting tools.
